## Method & honest limits

- **Model:** v6 calculator, full $225k down (condos ≤$225k all-cash), 6.25% fixed, owner-occupy 2 yrs then full rental; cash flow at Month 25; **neighborhood-specific economic vacancy (≈4.0–7.25% by submarket)** + 1% bad debt, 12% mgmt, scaled CapEx, $300 default condo HOA, 0.42% tax.
- **Vacancy is submarket-anchored to real Denver data**, not a flat assumption. Base rates come from the Apartment Association of Metro Denver / Apartment Insights survey (metro ~7.5%, City of Denver highest, SE Denver 9%+, Jeffco/Douglas lows ~5.8–6.5%), shifted down for SFH/condo/small-multifamily since those lease tighter than the new-apartment-tower headline. Tightest (~4.5%): premier + student areas (Wash Park, Platt Park, DU, Cherry Creek). Tight (~5%): close-in established (Cap Hill, Cheesman, Congress Park, Highlands, Park Hill, Edgewater). Moderate (~5.75%): Uptown, Baker, Lincoln Park, Five Points. Soft (~7%): downtown/RiNo towers competing with the new-build glut. A light type nudge (SFH −0.5pt, condo +0.25pt) sits on top. Per-property values are in `vacancy_assignments.csv`. Better-rentability areas keep more rent → higher cash flow AND net worth, so they rank higher without any artificial multiplier; the metric stays 420×CF + vs-investing.
- **Rents & HOAs are estimates** (per-ZIP rent adjustments on a bedroom schedule; multifamily configs inferred). Calculator math is exact; inputs carry error — verify any finalist with real comps and the real HOA before offering.
- **Coverage is broad but NOT a complete MLS.** Harvested from public structured listing pages; bounded by what search surfaces and by the price above which each type is deterministically worse than −$1k/mo (those never appear green). Condos & multifamily are well-covered; expensive single-family (mostly red) is sampled. To make it exhaustive, feed me an export (address, price, beds/units) and I'll run every row.
- **ZIPs:** 80211, 80212, 80205, 80218, 80203, 80206, 80209, 80210, 80223, 80204, 80202 (central) + 80221, 80207, 80220, 80214 (fringe). Snapshot only; listings change daily.
