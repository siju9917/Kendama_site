# What counts as a "critical" change?

BidDiff flags a change as **critical** when it meets at least one of these rules:

1. **Date or deadline** — the proposal due date, the period of performance,
   or any other deadline anchor changes.
2. **Page limit or required format** — Section L's "not to exceed N pages"
   or any mandatory format requirement changes.
3. **FAR / DFARS clause add or remove** — Section I gains or loses a
   regulation clause. New clauses can impose new compliance obligations.
4. **Evaluation criteria** — Section M factors, weighting, or basis for
   award.
5. **CLIN structure** — Section B adds, removes, or changes a Contract
   Line Item Number (quantity, unit, or description).
6. **Attachment add or remove** — Section J gains or loses an attachment.

Everything else (e.g. SOW prose tweaks, administrative wording) is shown
as a **normal** change so your team can still review it.

BidDiff identifies textual differences to assist professional review. It
does not provide legal, contracting, or capture advice — always confirm
any change with the original source documents.
