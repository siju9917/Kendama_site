# Visual Assets — Specs

## Icon

- 128×128, square, transparent or solid background.
- Mark: a simple geometric pair (two stacked horizontal bars) suggesting
  before/after — visible at 16×16. Color: BidDiff accent #1f5cd6 with
  white interior.
- Placeholder icons live in `/icons/`. Final branded icons replace them
  before submission.

## Promo tile

- **Small tile:** 440×280, dark text on white.
  - Headline: "Every amendment change. In 30 seconds."
  - Subhead: "Federal solicitation diff. On your device."
- **Marquee:** 1400×560.
  - Left half: product screenshot (side panel with one critical change card).
  - Right half: short value statement.

## Store screenshots (1280×800)

1. Side panel with the **Summary** card — total / critical / confidence,
   with a few category counts inline.
2. Side panel with a **critical change card** — clause add, with the
   plain-language note visible.
3. Side panel with a **MODIFY** card — token-level diff, words highlighted.
4. Settings page — license key, telemetry toggle, clear history.
5. The exported **PDF report** — header, summary, critical section.
