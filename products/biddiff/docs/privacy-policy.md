# BidDiff Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-06-05

BidDiff is a Chrome extension for proposal and capture professionals working on
U.S. federal solicitations. This policy explains what the extension does and does
not do with information that passes through it.

## Plain-language summary

- BidDiff processes your documents **entirely on your device**. It does not
  upload your solicitation files or amendments to any server. **There is no
  BidDiff server.**
- The **only** time information leaves your device is when *you* click "Compare
  with BidDiff" on a SAM.gov opportunity page: the extension then downloads that
  attachment from SAM.gov — the same file your browser would download anyway — so
  it can compare it locally.
- **No accounts. No license server. No telemetry. No tracking.** BidDiff does not
  phone home.

## What information BidDiff handles

1. **Solicitation documents you provide.** PDF and Word files you pick, drop in,
   or download from SAM.gov via the extension. Parsed and compared **locally** in
   your browser; stored locally on your device (extension storage / IndexedDB).
   You can clear them from the Settings page.
2. **The structured diff result.** The computed comparison between two versions.
   Stored locally on your device. You can clear it.
3. **Settings and local history.** Your export preferences and a local list of
   diffs you've run. Stored locally on your device only.

## What leaves your device

**Only one thing, and only when you initiate it:** when you click "Compare with
BidDiff" on a SAM.gov opportunity page, BidDiff fetches that attachment directly
from SAM.gov so it can be compared on your device. No document content is ever
sent to BidDiff or to any third party — because BidDiff operates no server.

## What BidDiff does **not** do

- It does not upload, transmit, or retain your solicitation files anywhere.
- It does not operate any license, telemetry, OCR, or analytics server.
- It does not read pages outside SAM.gov.
- It does not set cookies, use accounts, or track you.
- It does not sell or share data with third parties.
- It does not use document content to train any model.

## Cookies / tracking

None. The extension sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

## Children

BidDiff is intended for professional use by adults in their work. It is not
directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from
them.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes will be announced in the in-product Settings page and the Web
Store listing description before they take effect.
